# Always-on operations at $0

MailerLite hosts the landing page and processes confirmed signups without a local computer. Its group-triggered automation sends five educational emails at days 0, 2, 4, 7 and 14, once activated. Double opt-in and a visible consent checkbox protect subscriber choice. Keep the native unsubscribe link in every email.

The single scheduled GitHub workflow, money-maker.yml, runs every six hours to generate reviewable drafts. It does not publish content, enroll subscribers, send emails or earn commissions. Scheduled jobs may be delayed; this is not a continuously running server. The duplicate legacy workflow was removed to avoid redundant work and configuration logging. No paid model APIs are required.

MailerLite Free limits verified October 15, 2026: 250 active subscribers, 2,500 monthly emails, three active automations, three forms, one landing page and one website. Do not upgrade, add payment information, buy a domain, advertising or AI credits. Limits may pause service; check the account's current plan before changing usage.

Before activation: approve the sender mailing address displayed in subscriber emails; verify the PDF link; complete a genuine test signup, confirmation, delivery and unsubscribe. A test-email Sent result alone is not proof of inbox delivery or an end-to-end signup test. Do not count test subscriptions as customers or revenue.

MailerLite permits relevant, clearly disclosed affiliate links aligned with subscriber expectations; it prohibits unrelated commission-only promotions. Add only partner-approved tracking links separately approved by the owner. No affiliate links are in the initial educational funnel.

Evaluate actual confirmed signups, delivery, unsubscribes and partner-confirmed commissions. More frequent draft generation or email sends does not establish better performance.
