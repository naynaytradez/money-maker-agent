## Day 2 — Useful workflow
Subject: A 15-minute AI workflow experiment

## Day 4 — Picking the right tool
Subject: How to compare AI tools without the hype

Compare tools using five questions: Does it complete the task reliably? Can you review its output? What information does it collect? What does it cost? Does it integrate with your existing workflow? Avoid choosing a product solely because of commission or popularity.

## Day 7 — Common mistake
Subject: The biggest mistake in AI productivity

Automating an unclear process usually makes errors faster. Write down the steps and quality checks first, then automate a small, reversible portion. Treat AI outputs as drafts requiring review.
